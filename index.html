Heim Travel is a destination management company in Norway, planning incentive trips, meetings and events for companies. We begin with what yours has to accomplish and who you are bringing, then plan every day so they feel at home.

Inspiration, by purpose

A company event has a job to do. These are starting points, sorted by what you want yours to accomplish. The place comes later.

Heim means home.

It is the Norwegian word for home, and for the feeling of being there: you are expected, someone has thought of what you need, and you can be yourself.

That is the feeling we want to give the people you bring. We believe people who feel at home talk more openly, stay longer at the table and remember who they were with. Whether it is a kick-off, an incentive trip or a client event, it goes better that way.

So we start every project by listening. Before anyone picks a place, we want to know who is coming, why you are gathering them and what should be different afterwards. Norway is where we live and work. Where we take you depends on what we hear.
